How it works

From each family’s data to a booked visit

We start from each person’s records and published guidelines, then act. The employee approves every step.

Why it’s different

Navigators wait for a call. We act first.

Typical navigation service

  • Starts when the employee calls or chats
  • Works from claims, after care happened
  • Human advocates, priced for human labor

Care Navigator

  • Starts from each family’s records, before care is missed
  • A care plan for every family member
  • An AI agent books the visits; people handle exceptions
🔒 Built for trust. Employees approve every step. You see totals only, never an individual’s health information. HIPAA and SOC 2 before launch.
